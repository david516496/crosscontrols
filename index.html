What we design

New control panels

Enclosure sizing, backpanel layout, wire duct and DIN rail plans, heat load and door cutouts for new machines and process skids.

Schematics and wiring

Power and control schematics, PLC I/O drawings, terminal plans and wire numbering that match what goes on the backpanel.

Bills of material

Manufacturer part numbers, quantities and preferred suppliers in one spreadsheet, with spare parts called out so nothing stalls a build.

Motor control and VFDs

Starter and drive selection, line reactors, branch protection and cooling for pumps, conveyors and fans.

Retrofits and upgrades

Field walk-downs of existing panels, as-built drawings and a clean upgrade plan for obsolete PLCs, drives and relays.

Design reviews

A second set of eyes on drawings from your team or another vendor before parts are ordered and wire is cut.

A package your shop can build from

Every job is released as a complete, revision-controlled set in PDF and native files, with the BOM in Excel.

How a project runs

Walk-down or kickoff

We visit the machine or meet online, collect specs, loads, I/O lists and photos of what exists today.

First issue

Layout, schematics and BOM issued for review, usually as one PDF set you can mark up.

Markups and revisions

Your comments are folded in and each change is tracked in the revision block.

Release for build

Final package to your panel shop, with native files, the Excel BOM and label lists.
